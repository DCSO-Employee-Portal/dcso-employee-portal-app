DCSO EMPLOYEE PORTAL APP LAUNCHER

This is an installable web app launcher, NOT a native iOS/Android app and not yet deployed.

Portal destination: https://sites.google.com/dadesheriffworkspace.com/dcso-employee-portal

DEPLOYMENT
1. Host these files together at an HTTPS address under a department-controlled domain or trusted hosting provider.
2. Open the hosted index.html in Chrome on Android, then use Install app/Add to Home screen.
3. On iPhone, open the hosted index.html in Safari, tap Share > Add to Home Screen.
4. Tap the icon, then tap Open Employee Portal. The portal opens at Google Sites and Google Workspace permissions still apply.

LIMITATIONS
The launcher cannot install itself directly from this ZIP. Google Sites cannot host these custom manifest/service-worker files directly. The app opens the portal via a link, rather than embedding or replacing it. Browser login may be required. The launcher itself contains no confidential department data and does not bypass Google Workspace permissions.

For a simpler no-hosting alternative, employees can add the existing Google Sites portal URL directly to their home screen, but its icon may not be the custom badge.
